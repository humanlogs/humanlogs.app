Transcribing starts with **New transcription** on the home screen. This page covers what you can send us and what happens next; the settings on that screen are covered in [Advanced options](/docs/transcribe/advanced-options).

## What you can upload

**Audio** — MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC, OGG, Opus, WMA, AIFF.

**Video** — MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, FLV, WMV, MPEG, 3GP. Only the audio track is extracted and uploaded; the video itself never leaves your computer.

Files your browser can compress before uploading are accepted up to **4 GB**; anything uploaded as-is must stay under **300 MB**. In practice a two-hour interview recorded on a phone is well within both.

## Adding files

Drag files in, or click to browse. You can queue several recordings at once — each becomes its own document.

For each file you see its duration and size, and you can rename it before starting. That name becomes the document name, so `Interview 04 — teacher, rural school` beats `REC_0042.m4a` when you come back to it in six months.

## Before you start

The screen shows the total duration, the credits it will take and your balance. One credit is one minute of audio, rounded up per file, and you are told if the balance is short *before* you commit rather than halfway through. See [Credits](/docs/privacy/credits).

This is also where you set the language, the number of speakers and your custom vocabulary — three settings that decide most of the quality you will get. They are worth two minutes: see [Advanced options](/docs/transcribe/advanced-options).

## While it processes

Files are converted if needed, then uploaded, both with progress. After that, count roughly **ten minutes of processing per hour of audio**.

You can close the page. The work happens on our side, and we email you when the transcript is ready — the document appears in your sidebar either way.

## When a file fails

A failed document shows the reason and stays in your list. Nothing is lost, and **failures do not consume credits**.

- Does the file play in your own media player? A file that will not play locally will not transcribe.
- Is it silent? A muted input produces an empty transcript.
- Is it over 135 minutes on EU processing? Split it, or switch to US processing.
- Is it over 300 MB in a format the browser cannot convert? Re-export it as MP3 first.

Re-uploading solves most transient failures. If the same file fails twice, [contact us](/contact) with the document name — we can look at what the provider returned.

## Already have a transcript?

Do not pay to transcribe it again — switch to **Import a document** on the same screen. See [Import text only](/docs/transcribe/import-text).
