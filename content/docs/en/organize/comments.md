Comments are how a team discusses a transcript without touching it: a translation choice, a doubtful passage, a decision about anonymisation. The transcript stays what was said; the argument about it lives beside it.

## Leave a comment

Select the passage and use the comment button in the toolbar, or **⌘⌥M**. Commented lines are marked in the text and the thread appears in the rail on the right; arrows jump to the previous or next comment in the document.

Anyone with access can comment, whatever their role. Read-only collaborators included.

## Reply and mention

Threads take replies. Typing **@** offers the people the document is shared with, and mentioning someone notifies them directly, whether or not they follow the thread.

## Follow a thread

You follow a thread as soon as you take part in it, and you can follow or unfollow any other thread manually. Followers are notified of new replies.

## Editing and deleting

You can edit or delete your own comments. Deleting a comment removes the marker from the text and leaves the transcript untouched.

## What you are notified about

The bell in the app lists mentions and replies in threads you follow. Transcription completion is emailed to you, so you can close the page while a long recording is processed.
