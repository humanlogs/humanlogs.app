Transcription is metered in credits, and the unit is deliberately boring: **one credit is one minute of audio**.

## What consumes credits

Only transcription. A 47-minute interview costs 47 credits, rounded up per file.

Nothing else is metered. Editing, collaborating, commenting, exporting, sharing, storage and re-downloading your audio all cost nothing, whatever plan you are on. Correcting a transcript for three hours consumes no credits.

Importing an existing transcript is also free — no audio is processed. If you already have the text, do not pay to produce it again. See [Import an existing transcript](/docs/transcribe/import-text).

## Before you commit

The upload screen shows the total duration, the credits it will take and your current balance. If the balance is insufficient, you are told before starting rather than halfway through.

## Failures do not cost you

A transcription that fails does not consume credits. Re-uploading a file that failed is free — see [When transcription fails](/docs/transcribe/import-audio).

## How the balance refills

Your balance is topped up **monthly** to your plan's allowance:

- **Free** — 100 minutes per month.
- **Subscription** — 1,200 minutes (20 hours) per month.

The refill sets your balance to the allowance; it does not stack month on month. An unused free month does not become 200 minutes the next. Plan a heavy fieldwork period accordingly — or buy a one-time pack, whose minutes are not tied to a monthly cycle.

Referral bonuses are added to the monthly refill for as long as the referred accounts exist. See Referrals.

## Running out mid-project

Two options, no subscription required for either: buy a **one-time pack** (1,200 minutes, $20) or subscribe. Both are in your account's billing section — see Plans and invoices.

## A rough guide

For planning purposes, at one credit per minute:

| Material | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| A 30-minute interview | 30 |
| A 90-minute focus group | 90 |
| Ten 1-hour interviews | 600 |
| A 2-day conference recorded in full | ~1,000 |

The free tier covers roughly two to three interviews a month; a doctoral corpus of forty interviews fits in two months of a subscription.

Everything is priced in minutes of audio. One credit is one minute — see Credits.

## Free

100 minutes per month, no credit card. Every feature is included: the free tier is smaller, not weaker.

## Subscription

$15 per month (or $165 per year) for 1,200 minutes — 20 hours — per month, with priority processing.

## One-time pack

$20 for 1,200 minutes, with no subscription. The right choice for a fieldwork burst followed by six quiet months.

## Managing your subscription

The billing portal in your account handles payment method, cancellation and invoices. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the paid period; your documents are never held hostage by a lapsed subscription.

Invoices are downloadable from the same portal — that is what to give an accounting department.

## Referrals

You can invite up to **10** people by email, from onboarding or from **Account → Referral**. When someone you invited creates a new account, you get **15 credits immediately** and the same 15 credits added to your monthly refill, for as long as they stay. Ten active referrals means 150 extra minutes every month.

Pending invitations can be removed, which frees the slot for someone else. If a referred account is deleted, your monthly bonus is recalculated; credits already granted stay yours.

## For institutions

Universities, labs and newsrooms usually need more than a credit card. We can put in place institutional invoicing and purchase orders, a signed [DPA](/legal/dpa) with answers to your security questionnaire, and volume pricing for a department or research group.

If data cannot leave the institution at all, the answer is running HumanLogs yourself — see [Install](/docs/self-hosting/installation-guide). [Contact us](/contact) and say what your constraints are.
