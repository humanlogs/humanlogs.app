Transcription is metered in credits, and the unit is deliberately boring: **one credit is one minute of audio**.

## What consumes credits

Transcription, and nothing else. A 47-minute interview costs 47 credits, rounded up per file.

Editing, collaborating, commenting, exporting, sharing, storage and re-downloading your audio are all free, on every plan, forever. Correcting a transcript for three hours consumes nothing. [Importing an existing transcript](/docs/transcribe/import-text) is free too, no audio is processed.

A transcription that **fails does not consume credits**, and re-uploading the file is free.

The upload screen shows the duration, the credits it will take and your balance before you commit; if the balance is short, you are told there rather than halfway through.

## The plans

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minutes | 100 / month | 1,200 / month | 1,200 |

Every feature is included everywhere: the free tier is smaller, not weaker. End-to-end encryption, collaboration, every export format and the full editor are on all plans.

The subscription adds priority processing when the queue is busy.

## How the balance refills

Your balance is topped up **monthly** to your plan's allowance. The refill *sets* the balance to the allowance, it does not stack: an unused free month does not become 200 minutes the next.

Plan a heavy fieldwork period accordingly, or buy a one-time pack, whose minutes are not tied to a monthly cycle. Referral bonuses are added to the monthly refill for as long as the referred accounts exist.

## A rough guide

| Material | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| A 30-minute interview | 30 |
| A 90-minute focus group | 90 |
| Ten 1-hour interviews | 600 |
| A 2-day conference recorded in full | ~1,000 |

The free tier covers two to three interviews a month; a doctoral corpus of forty interviews fits in two months of subscription.

## Invoices and cancelling

The billing portal in your account handles payment method, cancellation and invoices, that last one is what an accounting department will ask for.

Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the paid period, and your documents are never held hostage by a lapsed subscription: you keep them, you can keep editing and exporting them, you simply stop getting new minutes.

## Referrals

You can invite up to **10** people by email, from onboarding or from **Account → Referral**. When someone you invited creates a new account, you get **15 credits immediately** and the same 15 added to your monthly refill for as long as they stay, ten active referrals means 150 extra minutes every month.

Pending invitations can be removed, which frees the slot. If a referred account is deleted, the monthly bonus is recalculated; credits already granted stay yours.

## Institutions

Universities, labs and newsrooms usually need more than a credit card: institutional invoicing and purchase orders, a signed [DPA](/legal/dpa), answers to a security questionnaire, volume pricing for a department. [Contact us](/contact) with your constraints.

If data cannot leave the institution at all, the answer is not a plan but [self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting/installation-guide), which is free and unmetered, you bring your own transcription provider.
