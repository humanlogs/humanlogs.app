HumanLogs turns interview recordings into transcripts you can actually work with: a transcript editor bound to the audio, speaker labels, collaboration with your team, and exports to the tools the rest of your analysis lives in.

It is built for research material, which is why privacy is a feature and not a footnote. Audio and transcripts can be end-to-end encrypted in your browser, the transcription providers we use apply zero-retention policies, and you can choose whether your audio is processed in the EU or in the US.

## Who it is for

Researchers, PhD students, journalists, podcasters and teachers — anyone who records people talking and then has to read, correct, quote and analyse what was said.

## Open source

HumanLogs is AGPL v3 and the same codebase runs the hosted service and any self-hosted install. If your institution cannot send recordings to a third party, see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting/installation-guide).
