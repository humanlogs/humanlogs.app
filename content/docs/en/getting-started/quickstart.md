This walks through one full interview, end to end. Nothing here needs a paid plan: the free tier includes 100 minutes a month and every feature.

## 1. Upload the recording

From the home screen, choose **New transcription** and drop your file in. Audio and video both work, for video, only the audio track is uploaded, so your browser does the extraction and a 2 GB screen recording does not become a 2 GB upload.

While the file is being read, the interface shows its duration and how many credits it will cost. One credit is one minute of audio.

## 2. Set three things before you start

Only three settings matter, and the defaults are usually right:

- **Language**: pick the language spoken in the recording. Getting this right matters more than any other setting.
- **Number of speakers**: if you know there were two people, say two. Detection is better when it is not guessing.
- **Custom vocabulary**: proper nouns, jargon, participant pseudonyms. A dozen words here removes a hundred corrections later.

If your instance offers both, you also choose whether the audio is processed in the **EU** or the **US**. See [Where your audio is processed](/docs/transcribe/advanced-options).

Then start the transcription.

## 3. Go do something else

Transcription takes about ten minutes per hour of audio. You can close the page, the work happens on our side and we email you when the transcript is ready.

## 4. Correct it with the audio

Open the document and you get the editor: the audio player above, the transcript below, speakers in the left column.

The one interaction to learn: **click any word and the audio jumps to that moment**. From there:

- **Space** or **Tab** plays and pauses. Inside the text, use **Alt/Ctrl + Space**.
- **Hold Alt** for half speed, **hold Ctrl** for double speed. Release to return to normal.
- Click a speaker name to rename them everywhere in the document, "Speaker 1" becomes "Interviewer" once.

Corrections save as you type. There is no save button and no version to lose.

A first pass on a one-hour interview usually takes 30 to 45 minutes. Working with the audio rather than against it is what makes the difference.

## 5. Share it, or export it

To work with someone: **Share**, enter their email, then pick what they can do (read, read and listen, or write). See [Share a document](/docs/organize/collaboration).

To take it elsewhere: **Download as…** and pick your format, Word and PDF for reading, NVivo or MAXQDA for coding, CSV or JSON for analysis, SRT or WebVTT for subtitles. See [Export formats](/docs/transcribe/export).
