## How accurate is the transcription?

It depends on the recording. Clean audio in a well-supported language usually needs light corrections; overlapping speakers, background noise and heavy accents need more. The editor is designed around the assumption that you *will* correct the transcript.

## How long does it take?

Roughly ten minutes per hour of audio. You can close the page — we email you when it is ready.

## Can I use my own recordings from Zoom, Teams or a dictaphone?

Yes. Any common audio or video file works — see [Supported files](/docs/transcribe/import-audio).

## Do you train AI models on my data?

No. The transcription providers we use apply zero-retention policies, and with end-to-end encryption enabled we cannot read your content at all.

## Can I get an invoice for my institution?

Yes, from the billing portal in your account. For institutional pricing or a signed DPA, [contact us](/contact).
