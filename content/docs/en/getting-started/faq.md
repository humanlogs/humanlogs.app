## How accurate is the transcription?

It depends on the recording. Clean audio in a well-supported language usually needs light corrections; overlapping speakers, background noise and heavy accents need more. The editor is built around the assumption that you *will* correct the transcript, which is the honest way to work with research material anyway.

Set the language, the number of speakers and a custom vocabulary before you start: those three do more for accuracy than anything you can do afterwards. See [Advanced options](/docs/transcribe/advanced-options).

## How long does it take?

Roughly ten minutes per hour of audio. You can close the page, we email you when it is ready.

## Can I use recordings from Zoom, Teams or a dictaphone?

Yes. Any common audio or video file works, and for video only the audio track is uploaded. See [Import audio](/docs/transcribe/import-audio).

## Do you train AI models on my data?

No. The transcription providers we use apply zero-retention policies, and with [end-to-end encryption](/docs/privacy/encryption) enabled we cannot read your content at all.

## Can several people work on the same transcript?

Yes, at the same time, with three levels of access, including read-without-the-audio, which is often exactly what an ethics protocol requires. See [Collaboration](/docs/organize/collaboration).

## Can I code and analyse my corpus here?

Not yet. [Codebooks](/docs/coding/codebooks) exist; applying codes to passages and [analysing](/docs/analysis/learn-more) a coded corpus are being built. Today you export to NVivo, MAXQDA or CSV and code there, see [Export](/docs/transcribe/export).

## What happens if I stop paying?

You keep your documents, and you can keep editing and exporting them. You simply stop receiving new minutes. See [Credits](/docs/privacy/credits).

## Can I get an invoice for my institution?

Yes, from the billing portal in your account. For institutional pricing, purchase orders or a signed DPA, [contact us](/contact).

## Can I run it on our own servers?

Yes, it is open source and self-hostable, and with a local Whisper server the recordings never leave your network. See the [installation guide](/docs/self-hosting/installation-guide).
