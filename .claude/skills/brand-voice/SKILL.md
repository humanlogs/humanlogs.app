# L'Inventaire — brand voice

How to sound like L'Inventaire when writing public content.

## Product facts (never invent beyond these)

- **L'Inventaire** (https://linventaire.app) is a "copilote de gestion" for
  invoicing, quotes, stock/inventory, accounting & reporting, CRM and French
  electronic invoicing (facturation électronique, Factur-X).
- It is **open source** (https://github.com/L-Inventaire/linventaire.app) and
  self-hostable; customer data is always **exportable** (portabilité des
  données).
- The hosted plan is **50€ HT/mois/utilisateur**. The app lives at
  https://app.linventaire.app.
- Do not invent other features, prices, integrations or legal claims. When in
  doubt about a product capability, check the feature pages under
  `src/app/fonctionnalites/` or leave it out.

## Audience & language

- Write in **French**, for French freelancers, auto-entrepreneurs and small
  businesses (TPE/PME). They are busy, not accountants, and allergic to jargon.
- Sign content as **L'Inventaire** (e.g. `author: "L'Inventaire"` in blog
  frontmatter).

## Tone

- Clear, concrete, helpful. Short sentences. Explain terms the first time they
  appear (e.g. PDP, Factur-X).
- No fluff, no grandiose openers ("dans un monde où…"), no empty
  superlatives.
- Be useful first: the reader should get real answers even if they never try
  the product.

## Product mentions & CTA

- Mention L'Inventaire where it genuinely helps the reader, typically in a
  closing section that connects the topic to the product (facturation, devis,
  stock, comptabilité, facturation électronique, open source /
  auto-hébergement).
- Stay factual and low-pressure — position against lock-in and complexity
  (open source, data portability, simplicity), never by disparaging
  competitors with invented claims.
