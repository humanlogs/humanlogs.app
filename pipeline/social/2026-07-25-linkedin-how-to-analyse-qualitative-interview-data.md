**Accroche**

Most guides to qualitative analysis treat "the data" as one generic blob. Interview transcripts aren't survey responses, and analysing them the same way misses what makes an interview worth doing in the first place.

**Texte**

A few things that are specific to analysing interview data:

• Meaning is distributed across the conversation, a quote pulled out of context (including the interviewer's question) can misrepresent what a participant meant
• Constant comparison across transcripts, not just within one, is what surfaces genuine patterns instead of coincidences
• A striking quote from one interview isn't a theme until it recurs across the corpus
• Saturation, not a sample size fixed in advance, is the real signal you have enough interviews
