**Texte**

A practical breakdown of what researchers actually use:

• NVivo and ATLAS.ti: the safe default for large or mixed-methods projects, powerful but a real learning curve
• MAXQDA: a middle ground, especially strong for survey data
• Delve and Dovetail: fast to learn, built for solo researchers and small teams
• Taguette: free, open source, and genuinely enough for a student project or pilot study
• Sometimes: a spreadsheet is all a small project needs

Plus what to check before you commit (price model, collaboration, mixed-methods support, data residency).
