**Accroche**

Three hundred open-ended survey responses in a spreadsheet is not the same problem as one interview transcript. Most qualitative methods guides don't say that out loud.

**Texte**

A few things that change when you're coding survey free-text instead of interview data:

• No follow-up questions, so be conservative about inferring meaning that isn't explicitly stated
• Deductive coding (from your research questions) beats fully inductive, line-by-line coding on short answers
• Counting how often a code appears is meaningful here, not just qualitative nuance
• AI-assisted coding is more defensible on short, self-contained responses than on nuanced interview dialogue, if you define the scheme yourself first and spot-check
