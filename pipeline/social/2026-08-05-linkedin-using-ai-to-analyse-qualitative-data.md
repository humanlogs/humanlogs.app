**Accroche**

"Can ChatGPT analyse qualitative data?" You can paste a transcript in and get something that looks like a thematic analysis in thirty seconds. Whether that belongs in a thesis is a different question.

**Texte**

Where AI genuinely helps with qualitative coding, and where it doesn't:

• Helps: first-pass code suggestions you review and correct
• Helps: summarising a long transcript to decide where to focus your close reading
• Helps: flagging patterns worth checking manually
• Doesn't replace: interpretive validity you can defend to a reviewer
• Doesn't replace: the reflexivity and context a trained researcher brings
• Watch out for: pasting participant transcripts into a consumer AI tool that wasn't built with a research data agreement in mind
